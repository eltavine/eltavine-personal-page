I'm Eltavine, a software engineer who likes to work close to the platform: Android internals, native code, and the infrastructure that keeps services running.

A lot of my work starts with a <Note text="where most projects start">trust question</Note>. Is this device in the state it claims? Is this config safe to roll out? Does this data need to leave the machine at all? I like answering those with <Highlight>tools that check locally and show their evidence</Highlight>.

I care about calm software: clear signals, honest status, and failure modes that are designed rather than discovered.
